### Notes
- introduces [[Satelite-based QKD]]
- based on [[LEO]] satellite
- [[decoy-state]] QKD
- over kHz key rate from the satellite to ground over a [[Distance]] up to 1200 km 
- 20 order of magnitudes more efficient than optical fiber with 0.2dB/km loss (does this mean that we measure in SNR)? is it [[Fiber CV]] ? )

### Limitations
- limited coverage area 
- amount of time spent in each ground station
